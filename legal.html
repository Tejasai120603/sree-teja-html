Rental and refund terms

Summary. The signed rental agreement governs.

Rent and deposit

  • Rent is paid monthly in advance by the 13th, with a 3 day grace period. A late charge applies after that.
  • GST is charged extra where applicable.
  • A refundable security deposit of one month’s rent is taken before delivery, without interest.

Deposit refund

We inspect returned equipment within 7 working days. The deposit is refunded within 7 working days after a satisfactory inspection, less any unpaid rent, repair or replacement cost.

Use and care

  • Equipment must run on a working UPS with proper earthing.
  • The renter bears the cost of physical damage, liquid damage and loss. Normal wear and tear is not charged.
  • Equipment stays at the delivery address and must not be opened, modified or sub-let.

Ending a rental

Either party may end a month-to-month rental with 30 days’ written notice. A lock-in period carries the early exit terms stated in the agreement.
